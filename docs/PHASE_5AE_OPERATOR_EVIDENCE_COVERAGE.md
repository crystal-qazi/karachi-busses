# Phase 5AE — Operator Evidence Coverage Register

## Purpose

Record what publicly accessible SMTA sources actually establish for the nine historical Red People Bus route codes and five EV routes. This is a source-coverage register, not a route approval.

## Findings

- SMTA's People Bus Service page lists a general Route Alignment attachment. Retrieval of the attachment timed out during this review, so its contents have not been interpreted.
- The SMTA 2021 project announcement gives initial endpoints and distances for Route 1 (Model Colony–Tower, 29.5 km) and Route 3 (Nagan Chowrangi–Singer Chowrangi, 33 km). It does not publish the intermediate stop sequence in that announcement.
- SMTA's individual EV route pages publish numbered sequences: EV-01 18 stops, EV-02 8, EV-03 16, EV-04 10, EV-05 10. The pages also label these routes Active. Published sequence is not proof of present-day physical boarding coordinates or a live dispatch.
- For R2, R4, R8, R11–R14, this review has not retrieved a route-specific stop schedule from an accessible individual official page. The general alignment attachment remains the next source to obtain.

## Outputs

- `data/research/PHASE_5AE_OPERATOR_EVIDENCE_COVERAGE.csv`

## Safety

No historical candidate is promoted to a verified physical stop by this register. No coordinates are assigned, no route sequence is rewritten, and no route geometry is generated. OSM remains the primary geographic base once stop identities and boarding points are verified.
