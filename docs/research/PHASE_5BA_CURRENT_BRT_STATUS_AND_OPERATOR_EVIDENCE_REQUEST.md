# Phase 5BA — Current BRT status reconciliation and operator evidence request

**Review date:** 18 November 2026  
**Status:** Current published service-status evidence reconciled; physical station/stop inventory and approved geometry still outstanding. No database changes.

## 1. Executive decision

This phase updates BRT status evidence using currently accessible Government of Sindh, Provincial Assembly, PIDCL and SMTA publications. It does not approve new stop coordinates, station IDs, route shapes or database statuses.

- **Green Line:** Government of Sindh currently describes an approximately 22 km corridor from Surjani Town to central Karachi. PIDCL records the 22.5 km infrastructure phase as complete (2022) and a separate 4 km operationalisation/ITS phase as complete (2024). Sindh's FY2025–26 Budget White Paper reports Green Line ridership of about 60,000 passengers/day in May 2025. These support an operating corridor, but none supplies a current, operator-issued station-ID schedule with ordered boarding platforms.
- **Orange Line:** A 19 May 2026 Provincial Assembly answer states that a 4 km service is operating from TMA Office Orangi Town to Jinnah University for Women near Matriculation Board Office, with approximately 2,500 passengers/day. A 29 January 2026 Assembly answer reports the same endpoints and approximately 5,000 passengers/day. PIDCL records Orange Line operationalisation/ITS and Green Line integration works as 100% complete in 2025. However, the current SMTA home/project overview labels Orange Line as a planned addition. Treat the latest dated Assembly statement as direct evidence of reported operation, while preserving the SMTA website status inconsistency and passenger-count discrepancy.
- **Orange Line station count:** Published records differ: SMTA project text says four BRT stations plus one depot; the 2021 facility agreement says approximately four stations on the dedicated TMA–Board Office corridor and lists OL-1 to OL-4; the Sindh Transport Department has reported five bus stations. The Assembly answer supplies endpoints, not a complete stop list. Do not invent a fifth stop or treat the depot as a passenger station.
- **Green Line station count:** The 2016 PIDCL progress presentation states 25 stations but prints 29 named corridor entries. A current Government of Sindh page states approximate corridor length, not station count or ordered station names. Passenger-facing 22-stop lists remain secondary sources and are not upgraded to operator-confirmed data.

## 3. Orange Line interpretation

Keep these as separate evidence records:

1. **Reported operating passenger corridor (Assembly, 19-May-2026):** TMA Office Orangi Town to Jinnah University for Women near Matriculation Board Office, stated as 4 km.
2. **Dedicated contractual BRT corridor (2021 agreement):** TMA Office Orangi Town to Board Office, approximately 4 km, with approximately four bus stations.
3. **Contractual station IDs:** OL-1 5 No. Chowrangi; OL-2 Orangi Town Police Station; OL-3 Abdullah College; OL-4 Dilpasand Sweets.
4. **Mixed-traffic continuation:** Agreement describes buses continuing beyond Board Office in mixed traffic toward Hydri/Green Line and Nagan before turning; this must not be mistaken for four additional Orange Line BRT stations.
5. **Government project scope:** SMTA project page describes TMA Office to Jinnah University and states four BRT stations plus one depot. Its displayed “88 Kilometers” alignment length is inconsistent with the 4 km Assembly statement and should be treated as a likely page/data-entry error until corrected by the authority, not used as a route length.
6. **Unresolved count:** Sindh Transport Department material has reported five bus stations. No source reviewed here identifies a fifth named passenger station or maps it to a contract ID.

**Decision:** Keep OL-1 to OL-4 as contract identifiers only. Store TMA Office and Jinnah University as government-reported corridor endpoints, not as a complete ordered stop register. No fifth station inferred.

## 4. Green Line interpretation

- The 2016 PIDCL presentation reports “No. of Stations: 25 Nos” and shows a separate list of 29 names from KESC Power House through Municipal Park. Preserve the count and printed name list as separate fields.
- The 2017 construction drawing title block identifies the Green Line BRT station construction project from KESC Power House Chowrangi, Surjani to Municipal Park, Saddar. This is engineering scope evidence, not proof that all endpoints/landmarks are current passenger stops.
- The Government of Sindh's current Green Line overview says approximately 22 km from Surjani Town to central Karachi/Saddar but publishes no ordered station schedule.
- PIDCL's current portfolio shows the 22.5 km infrastructure phase completed in 2022 and a separate 4 km operationalisation/ITS package completed in 2024.
- The FY2025–26 Budget White Paper reports Green Line passenger ridership after transfer to SMTA. This is operational context, not a station list.
- The commonly published 22-stop passenger lists differ. Do not merge them, choose one, or use them to approve physical map markers.

**Decision:** Green Line operation/corridor is supported by government publications, but station identity, order, platform side, current extension and coordinates remain unapproved.

## 7. Evidence required to close the remaining blocker

- Current dated operator route sheets for R1–R4, R8, R11–R14 and EV-01–EV-05, including both directions and route variants.
- Green Line current station inventory and Orange Line current passenger stop/station inventory.
- Unique stop/station identifiers and authoritative WGS84 coordinates, with platform/road-side information.
- Operator-approved road-following route geometry or written permission to derive and redistribute geometry from supplied source data.
- A clear effective date and operational status for each route and stop.

A general route page or corridor schematic is not enough to verify a physical boarding point. A name match, proximity to a KML line, or historical Google Maps coordinate is not enough either.

- Keep all data sources pending until the project-specific verification process is satisfied.
- Keep route shapes empty until approved geometry and reuse rights are established.
- Do not activate a route solely because a government page labels it active; the project requires verified ordered stops, directions, physical stop coordinates/boarding sides, provenance and road-following shape.
- No database writes, imports, status changes, coordinate changes, route activation or public API changes were made in this phase.
