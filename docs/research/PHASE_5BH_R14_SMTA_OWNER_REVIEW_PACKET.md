## Source finding

The adopted Think Transportation route inventory and supplied KML cover R1–R4, R8–R13 and EV-01–EV-05; they do not provide an R14 route sequence. Do not fabricate a Think Transportation R14 sequence or infer one from another route.

SMTA's official Karachi route-map directory does publish R14 as **Maymar to Tower**, with the following via-description:

> Maymar to Tower via Nawaz Sharif Park, Maymar Ext, Maymar Mor, Ahsanabad, Jamali Pull, Al-Asif, Sorab Goth, Ayesha Manzil, Lalukhait, Teen Hatti, Jahangir Road, Guru Mandar, Numaish, Taj Complex, Sindh High Court, Tower.

Source: [Sindh Mass Transit Authority — Karachi Bus Route Map](https://smta.gos.pk/route-map), accessed 19 November 2026.

This is an SMTA-attributed sequence, not a Think Transportation sequence. The SMTA directory's via-description is the source basis for the review list below. Third-party route lists and videos may show a different or newer pattern; they are not silently substituted for the official SMTA text.
