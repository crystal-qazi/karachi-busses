# Phase 5AR — Current Green and Orange Line source reconciliation

**Review date:** 2026-11-18  
**Status:** New current-source evidence added. No final Green Line 25-station sequence is approved. Orange Line operating corridor endpoints/status are better supported by the 19 May 2026 Provincial Assembly answer, but the four contract station identities still require current operator confirmation.

## Green Line

### Strongest station-count evidence

The 19 March 2021 SIDCL response to pre-bid queries confirms **25 bus stations** on the Green Line project corridor. The 2016 Government/PIDCL progress presentation also reports 25 stations but names 29 corridor points in its sequence. The 2021 response confirms the count; it does not identify which four of the 29 named points are not passenger stations.

Sources:
- [SIDCL/PIDCL response to pre-bid queries, 19 March 2021](https://pidcl.com.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-19-Response-Document-Final-Version-.pdf)
- [Government/PIDCL Green Line progress presentation, 16 August 2016](https://pidcl.com.pk/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Updated-Progress-Presentation-Green-Line-16-8-16.pdf)

### New 2026 passenger-facing lists conflict

Two recently indexed passenger-facing pages both describe a 22-stop Green Line between Numaish and Abdullah Chowk/Surjani, but their named station lists differ materially:

- [BusUpdate Green Line list](https://busupdate.com/pakistan/karachi/bus-routes/route-green-line/) includes Erum Shopping, Jummah Bazar, Five Star Chowrangi, Hyderi, Board Office, Annu Bhai Park, Enquiry Office and Nazimabad No. 1.
- [CMTA Green Line list](https://cmta.com.pk/karachi-green-line-brt-route/) instead lists Lasbela, Gul Bahar, Urdu Bazaar, Eid Gah Bagh, North Nazimabad, Orange Line Interchange, Shahrah-e-Humayun, Shahrah-e-Jehangir, Sakhi Hassan and other entries not present in the BusUpdate list.

These are not interchangeable lists. Neither is an operator station-ID schedule. The 22-stop passenger-facing lists also do not reconcile the 25-station project count or the 29 historical corridor points.

### Current extension scope

A Dawn report dated 18 October 2026 says the Green Line extension from Numaish Chowrangi to Municipal Park near Jama Cloth Market is expected to complete during FY 2026–27. Therefore, retain Numaish as the currently reported passenger-facing city-end terminal in the 22-stop list, while recording Municipal Park as an extension project endpoint—not as an already operating passenger station based on this report.

Source: [Dawn, 18 October 2026](https://www.dawn.com/news/2037938/karachis-green-line-extension-to-be-completed-in-fy27-assures-governor).

### Green Line decision

- Keep the 25-station count as the project-count evidence.
- Keep the 29 named 2016 points as historical planning/corridor references.
- Keep both 22-stop 2026 passenger lists as separate secondary-source variants.
- Do not choose one list or delete four points by inference.
- Do not assign station coordinates or route geometry until an operator-issued station schedule/drawing with station IDs resolves the versions.

## Orange Line

### Current operating-corridor evidence

The Provincial Assembly of Sindh answer for the sitting of **19 May 2026** states that the BRT Orange Line (4 km) is in operation from Town Municipal Administration Office, Orangi Town to Jinnah University for Women near Matriculation Board Office, serving approximately 2,500 passengers daily.

Source: [Provincial Assembly of Sindh, question 520, 19 May 2026](https://pas.gov.pk/business/questios-details/33/2019).

This recent government statement is stronger evidence of current operation than the current SMTA homepage's generic "planned addition" wording. Retain both statements with their dates; do not describe the line as merely planned without qualification.

### Four contract station identities

The 2021 Orange Line Facility Management Agreement Exhibit 1 lists:
1. OL-1 — 5 No. Chowrangi
2. OL-2 — Orangi Town Police Station
3. OL-3 — Abdullah College
4. OL-4 — Dilpasand Sweets

The contract and SMTA project page distinguish the four-station dedicated corridor from mixed-traffic/Green Line connection segments and use differing length scopes. The 2026 Assembly answer confirms endpoints and operation, but does not re-confirm these four station names, station IDs, or their current order.

Sources:
- [Orange Line Facility Management Agreement (2021)](https://pidcl.com.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Orange-Line-Project-Facility-Management-Agreement_compressed.pdf)
- [SMTA Orange Line project page](https://smta.sindh.gov.pk/brt-abdul-sattar-edhi-line-orange-line)

### Orange Line decision

- Accept TMA Office Orangi to Jinnah University for Women near Matriculation Board Office as the government-reported operating corridor endpoints (19 May 2026).
- Retain OL-1 to OL-4 as contract station identities, not as newly operator-confirmed current stops.
- Do not add Board Office, Hydri or Nagan as extra Orange Line passenger stations merely because they occur in the wider mixed-traffic/shared-corridor contract scope.
- Do not build route geometry until the current operator confirms the four station IDs, sequence, boarding points and whether the mixed-traffic extension is still operated.
