# Phase 5V — Green Line station-count confirmation

**Finding:** The 24/25 station-count discrepancy is resolved in favour of **25 bus stations** by the 19 March 2021 SIDCL response to pre-bid queries.

The employer's response explicitly says there are twenty-five (25) bus stations on the Green Line BRTS project corridor, with approximately 900 metres average spacing, and that the RFP reference should be corrected to twenty-five.

Source: [SIDCL / PIDCL response document, 19 March 2021](https://pidcl.com.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-19-Response-Document-Final-Version-.pdf), query 3.

## What this resolves

- The Green Line station count is 25, not 24.
- The 2016 progress presentation's count of 25 is consistent with the 2021 employer response.

## What remains unresolved

The 2016 presentation names 29 points in its corridor sequence. The 2021 response confirms the count but does not list the 25 station names or map the four non-station/corridor labels. Therefore, it does **not** prove which four of the 29 named points are not stations, nor does it supply a current direction-specific passenger sequence or station IDs.

Do not delete four names or choose a 25-stop subset by guesswork. Keep all 29 historical points as planning-corridor references until an official station schedule/drawing identifies the 25 stations.

No coordinates, route geometry, current operating status or passenger boarding locations are approved by this evidence. OSM remains the primary geographic base for later mapping.
