## Draft email

**Subject:** Request for official route, stop, and BRT station data for Karachi public transport information project

Dear Managing Director / Director Bus Operations,

I am developing an independent public-information and research application for Karachi public transport. I am conducting a source-attributed verification of published Peoples Bus Service, Pink Bus, EV Bus, Green Line and Orange Line route information.

The objective is to avoid publishing incorrect routes, stop locations, boarding sides, or station sequences. I would be grateful if your office could provide the latest approved records, or direct this request to the relevant data custodian.

### A. Peoples Bus / Pink Bus / EV Bus

For routes R1–R4, R8, R11–R14 and EV-01–EV-05, please provide:

1. Current approved route code/name, operator, service status and effective date.
2. Origin and destination terminal names, including any branches or extensions.
3. Ordered stop/landmark list for each direction, with separate return-direction sequence.
4. Official stop IDs, if available.
5. Stop coordinates (WGS84 latitude/longitude), road name, carriageway/boarding side, and platform/bay where recorded.
6. Current route map, route sheet, timetable or other approved document.
7. Clarification of the following discrepancies:
   - R2–R4: Powerhouse vs North Karachi origin and the published terminal differences.
   - R8: direction/orientation in the route index versus detailed route page.
   - R11: Mera Naka–Ziauddin Hospital versus Miran Naka–Shireen Jinnah Colony.
   - R12: Naddi Kinara–Lucky Star versus Memon Goth–Sadar Lucky Star.
   - R1 and R13: repeated or differently ordered place names in published descriptions.

### B. Green Line and Orange Line BRT

Please provide the current approved passenger station register for each line, including:

1. Official station ID and exact public-facing station name.
2. Ordered station sequence in both directions.
3. Current operational status and effective date of each station/extension.
4. Station entrance/platform coordinates and accessible interchange information.
5. Approved route alignment or GIS data, if releasable.

For Orange Line, please confirm the current operating corridor and the exact names/order of OL-1 through OL-4.

### C. Preferred data formats

If available, GTFS (routes, trips, stops, stop_times, shapes), GeoJSON, Shapefile, KML, Excel or CSV would be very helpful. PDF route sheets or dated official maps are also acceptable.

Please identify the publication/effective date, issuing department, and any usage or attribution conditions for the supplied data. If coordinates or internal operational details cannot be shared, an approved public route map or stop list would still be valuable.

I will retain source attribution, distinguish official published information from independently surveyed candidate data, and avoid representing unconfirmed records as verified.
