## Scope and method

Search-indexed individual route pages were used to extract visible route metadata and stop sequences. Therefore this is a substantial research pass, not proof that every listing on the live index has been exhaustively enumerated. Search results can be stale. Keep Mnzil as a secondary source and preserve its exact route page URL per record.

## Critical cross-source findings against SMTA register
1. Mnzil R-01 says 44 stops, 28 km, Khokhrapar to Dockyard; SMTA directory reports 31 km and includes a via list with Khokharapar 04, Suadabad, Model Colony and other landmarks. Need reconcile route shape/variant and counting.
2. Mnzil R-03 says 40 stops, 31 km, Power House to Nasir Jump; SMTA R3 directory reports North Karachi to Nasir Jump, 32 km, and a much shorter via list. Strong conflict requiring route alignment review.
3. Mnzil R-04 says 35 stops, 21 km, Power House to Jackson Market; SMTA says Powerhouse to Keamari, 26 km, via a shorter list. Likely different granularity/stop inventories; confirm exact alignment and endpoint.
4. Mnzil R-10 says 26 stops and 28 km; SMTA directory says 30 km and a different stop/via list.
5. Mnzil R-14 says 28 stops, 27 km; SMTA directory says 29 km.
6. EV-01: Mnzil says 30 stops/28 km, SMTA directory says 41 km and shorter via list.
7. EV-02: Mnzil says 10 stops/30 km and gives operating windows; SMTA says 44 km and 8 via points.
8. EV-03: Mnzil says 14 stops/20 km, SMTA says 23 km and different start wording/stop list.
9. Mnzil has many traditional/private bus route labels not present in SMTA's 20-route public service directory: D-1, D-7, G-11, G-19, M-55, A-25, P-3, W-11, 51, 7-Star, Khan, Bilal, MashaAllah, N. These should be maintained as a separate traditional/local bus research category, not silently mixed into government services.
10. Mnzil describes its collection as manually curated and acknowledges initial imperfections. All records are secondary-source-only pending independent corroboration.
