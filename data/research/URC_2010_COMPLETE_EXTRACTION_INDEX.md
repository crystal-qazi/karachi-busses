# URC Karachi Bus Routes — comprehensive text extraction register

**Source:** Urban Resource Centre, *Bus Routes Karachi*, January 2010  
**PDF:** https://urckarachi.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Bus-Routes-Karachi.pdf  
**Source checked:** 2026-11-16  
**Extraction basis:** searchable PDF text, 1,173 extracted text lines, 39 PDF pages.  
**Status:** historical research only; not current-service validation and not imported into any database.

## Extraction method and evidence handling
- Read the searchable text from the beginning to the end of the 1,173-line extraction, in overlapping sections to avoid gaps.
- Captured route identifiers and ordered stop strings as published. The source's original spelling, punctuation and apparent OCR errors are intentionally retained where practical.
- The extracted text does not reliably preserve PDF page boundaries. Therefore, **PDF page number is not fabricated**. The source text line range is recorded for locating the route in the searchable PDF; page-by-page visual QA remains pending.
- Some entries are truncated in the source text itself or at a page/column boundary. They are marked incomplete rather than guessed.
- Duplicate route identifiers are separate source variants, not duplicates to delete.
- Routes in this document are historical; do not infer that a route still operates.

## Data-quality issues found in the extracted text
1. OCR/transcription spellings vary: e.g. Lasbella/Lasbela, Nagan/Nagan Chowrangi, Sohrab/Sorab Goth, Keamari/Kemari, Khokrapar/Khokhrapar, Numaish/Numaish, Liaquataabad/Liaquatabad.
2. Some entries are visibly truncated: e.g. Baraq Coach, Ayaz Coach, Azmat Coach, City Coach, Ghulam Coach, Gulistan Coach, H-2, Filly Coach, F-22, Rainbow Coach, Star Line Coach, Super Barki Coach, Tariq Coach, Waziristan Coach.
3. Some records contain OCR joining errors and split lines; examples include “Bara Maidan” split across lines, “Bawany textile mills”, “Malir malt”, and other inconsistent terms.
4. Some routes have repeated places, loops or apparent return-to-origin paths. Do not deduplicate repeated stop strings without checking the source.
5. Route codes are not globally unique across operators or time. Keep a source-specific historical record ID, route label, variant number and source document.
6. The source does not consistently identify operator, vehicle class, route direction, service status, fares or schedule.
7. Landmark references are not necessarily formal stops or exact boarding locations.

## Remaining page-by-page QA
- Open each of the 39 PDF page images and compare OCR text against the visible page.
- Assign the exact printed PDF page number to every route record.
- Repair only obvious OCR artefacts, retaining original text in a separate field.
- Mark illegible or cut-off text as `incomplete`; never infer missing stops.
- Split every route into ordered stop rows only after its raw sequence has been checked.
- Compare historical route corridors against SMTA and Mnzil while retaining separate source records.
- Do not import into MySQL or publish to passengers until the user has reviewed and approved the research.
