# URC Karachi — historical bus route research

**Source:** Urban Resource Centre (URC) Karachi, *Bus Routes Karachi* PDF  
**Source URL:** https://urckarachi.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Bus-Routes-Karachi.pdf  
**Document's own date:** January 2010 (shown on page 1)  
**Length:** 39 pages  
**Research checked:** 2026-11-16  
**Use:** historical discovery and cross-checking only.  
**Database status:** NOT IMPORTED. Do not treat these as current services.

## What this source contains
A large text directory of Karachi bus/minibus/coach route identifiers and ordered stop descriptions. It includes numeric routes and variants (e.g. 1-A, 4-S, 17-J, 50-C), letter/number series (A-, B-, C-, D-, F-), and named coaches (e.g. Abaseen Coach, Bilal Coach, Data Coach). It is useful for historic corridor coverage, landmark aliases and finding possible road connections missing from contemporary public transport sources.

## Important limitations
- The route directory is dated January 2010; it is not evidence of present-day service operation.
- No dependable GPS stop coordinates or road-centreline geometry are supplied in the extracted text.
- Many names are landmarks, institutions, markets, neighbourhoods or road names, not necessarily formal boarding stop names.
- The document contains apparent duplicate route codes with different sequences (examples: 16-F, 17-I, 17-J, 18-K, 5-A); preserve each occurrence as a separate source variant until investigated.
- OCR/text extraction contains spelling errors, inconsistent transliteration, line-wrap artefacts and occasional incomplete sequences. Always check original PDF page before normalizing.
- Route order is the order printed in the document, not necessarily confirmed directionality or full reverse-direction sequence.
- No current status, frequency, fare, operator, fleet type or timetable should be inferred.

## Research value when compared with SMTA and Mnzil
- Historic route families reveal many more local corridors and route variants than the contemporary SMTA public route directory.
- Some names and alignments overlap with modern Mnzil entries (for example 7-Star / 7 Star Flying Coach, D-1, D-7, A-25, and several routes through Orangi, Sohrab Goth, Malir, Korangi, Numaish and Tower). Similar route labels do not prove the same service or same alignment.
- Repeated major corridors include: Surjani/North Karachi–Nagan–Sohrab Goth; Orangi–Banaras–SITE–Shershah–Tower; Malir/Landhi/Korangi–Drigh Road–Karsaz–Numaish; and University Road/NIPA–Gulshan–Saddar/Tower.
- These corridors are useful candidates for road-network research, not evidence that any specific bus currently runs them.
